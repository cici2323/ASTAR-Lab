PhD

Doctoral students

Funded

Fully funded positions for students with a background in robotics, computer vision, machine learning, or civil and architectural engineering. Strong programming matters more than a specific degree title, and so does a willingness to debug hardware in a cold parking lot.

Email the PI with your CV, a short paragraph on what you want to work on, and transcripts if you have them. Mention a paper of ours you actually read.

Master's researchers

Thesis & project

Thesis and project slots alongside the doctoral team. Good fit if you want a concrete engineering contribution on your record: a working perception module, a hardware integration, a field study.

UG

Undergraduate assistants

Syracuse students

Syracuse undergraduates can join for perception, hardware and field-testing work. No prior research experience required. Bring one skill you are already decent at, whether that is Python, CAD, wiring or writing.

Open

Collaborators, visitors and postdocs

By conversation

No standing postdoc advertisement, but we are open to conversations with researchers, visiting scholars and industry partners, especially contractors willing to let robots onto a real site.
