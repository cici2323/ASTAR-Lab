PhD

PhD Students

Funded positions

We welcome applicants with backgrounds in robotics, computer vision, machine learning, artificial intelligence, control, civil and architectural engineering, or related field. We especially value strong programming and problem-solving skills, hands-on experience with robotic systems, and an interest in deploying research in real construction environments.

To inquire about PhD opportunities, please email Prof. Yizhi Liu with your CV, transcripts, a brief description of your research interests, and how they connect to the lab's current work.

MS

Master's Students

Thesis & research projects

Master's students can participate in thesis and research projects alongside the lab's doctoral researchers. Projects may involve robot perception, machine learning, path planning, teleoperation, human-robot collaboration, sensing, hardware integration, or field experiments.

UG

Undergraduate Researchers

Syracuse University students

Syracuse undergraduate students interested in research are welcome to contact us. Opportunities may involve programming, computer vision, machine learning, robotics hardware, sensing, data collection, and field experiments. Prior research experience is not required, but curiosity, reliability, and a willingness to learn are important.

Open

Postdocs, Visitors & Collaborators

Opportunities by inquiry

We welcome inquiries from prospective postdoctoral researchers, visiting scholars, academic collaborators, and industry partners whose interests align with our research. We are particularly interested in collaborations that enable the development and evaluation of robotic technologies in real construction environments.
