Yizhi Liu

Assistant Professor · Civil & Environmental Engineering

Dr. Yizhi Liu is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Syracuse University. He holds a Ph.D. in Architectural Engineering from Penn State; three master's degrees, including an M.S. in Robotics from Columbia University, an M.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from Penn State, and an M.S. in Civil Engineering from the University of Michigan; a Graduate Certificate in Data Science; and a B.Eng. in Civil Engineering and Management from Tianjin University.

Dr. Liu directs the AI-driven Sensing Technology and Robotics (A-STAR) Lab, which develops advanced technologies to address critical challenges facing the civil and construction industries, including labor shortages, stagnant productivity, and persistent safety risks. His research integrates robotics, sensing technology, and artificial intelligence, with a current emphasis on developing AI-enabled robotic systems that assist workers in performing complex construction and infrastructure tasks in dynamic and hazardous built environments.

To date, Dr. Liu has published more than 40 peer-reviewed research papers in leading journals and conferences in construction automation, robotics, and related fields. He currently serves as PI or Co-PI on research projects totaling over $2 million in funding from organizations including the National Science Foundation (NSF), the NVIDIA Academic Grant Program, the New York State Energy Research and Development Authority (NYSERDA), and the New York Tech Hub Resources & Innovation Vouchers for Enterprises (NY THRIVE).
