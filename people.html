Yizhi Liu

Assistant Professor · Civil & Environmental Engineering

Yizhi Liu joined the College of Engineering and Computer Science at Syracuse University in 2024 and founded the A-STAR Lab. He holds a Ph.D. in architectural engineering from Pennsylvania State University, along with master's degrees in electrical engineering, robotics and civil engineering.

His research covers construction robotics, construction automation and human–robot collaboration, with earlier work on physiological computing and brainwave-driven control of robots on site.
