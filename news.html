Aug 2026

A humanoid that learns construction skills from workers

Paper

Yizhi Liu and Ph.D. student Yanxi Liu report a vision-based perception-and-action framework that lets a humanoid observe skilled construction workers and reproduce their technique on the task itself. Published in Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering.

Selected for the NVIDIA Academic Grant Program

Grant

The award supports autonomous infrastructure inspection with RTX PRO 6000 GPUs and Jetson AGX Orin modules, which together carry the pipeline from GPU-accelerated simulation to deployment on the robot.

Oct 2025

NSF Foundational Research in Robotics award

Grant

Supports development of an aerial-legged robot that takes off like a drone, lands on a roof, then switches to legged locomotion. Camera, LiDAR and tactile sensing are fused so the robot can inspect closely without a person on the ladder.
